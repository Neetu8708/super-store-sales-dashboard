# Super Store Sales Dashboard

## About the Project

I created this Sales Dashboard in Power BI using the Super Store dataset. The main purpose of the project is to understand sales and profit performance and to look at the data from different angles such as category, customer segment, payment mode, shipping mode, location, and monthly trends.

I cleaned and prepared the data and then created different visuals and measures in Power BI to make the dashboard interactive and easy to understand.

## Key Numbers

- Total Sales: 1.57M
- Total Profit: 175K
- Total Quantity: 22K
- Average Delivery: 4 days
- Profit Margin: 11.19%

## What the Dashboard Shows

- Sales by Category
- Top Sub-Categories by Sales
- Sales by Ship Mode
- Sales by State
- Sales by Customer Segment
- Sales by Payment Mode
- Monthly Sales by Year
- Monthly Profit by Year
- Region-wise analysis using a slicer

## Tools Used

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Excel

## Key Insights

- Office Supplies has the highest sales among the three main categories.
- Consumer is the largest customer segment, contributing around 48% of total sales.
- COD is the most common payment mode, contributing around 43% of sales.
- Phones is one of the top-performing sub-categories by sales.
- The overall profit margin is around 11.19%.
- The dashboard can be used to compare sales and profit trends across different regions and years.
